Good questions

Frequently asked

How often should I service my AC in Houston?

Twice a year: once in spring before the heat arrives and once in fall before heating season. Houston AC systems run almost year-round, so regular tune-ups make a big difference in efficiency and lifespan.

Should I repair or replace my system?

If your system is under about 10–12 years old and the repair is reasonable, repairing usually makes sense. We'll show you both options side by side, with honest numbers.

Do you offer financing?

Coming soon! We’re setting up financing options for new systems. In the meantime, ask about it when you request an estimate and we’ll keep you posted.

What areas do you serve?

All of Greater Houston. Our North Houston Home Base covers Spring, The Woodlands, Humble, Kingwood, Cypress and nearby areas. Our Sienna Home Base covers Missouri City, Sugar Land, Richmond, Pearland and Fort Bend County.

What's the fastest way to reach you?

Call or text us at (346) 268-7833. For non-urgent requests, the online appointment form works great, and we'll confirm quickly.
